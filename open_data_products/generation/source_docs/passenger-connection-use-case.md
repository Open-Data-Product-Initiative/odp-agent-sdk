# Passenger Connection Protection

Passenger Connection Protection helps airport operations and airline station
managers identify transfer passengers at risk of missing outbound flights. The
main users are connection desk coordinators, duty managers, airline station
managers, and passenger service supervisors.

The workflow starts when an inbound flight is estimated to arrive late or when a
terminal transfer route becomes congested. Users review connection risk by
inbound flight, outbound flight, terminal pair, gate distance band, passenger
count, and boarding close time.

The use case depends on the Flight Connection Reliability Data Product and the
Passenger Flow and Queue Data Product. It can also use selected fields from the
Airport Operations Performance Data Product when departure delay risk affects
whether an outbound flight can wait.

Supported decisions include holding boarding for a short interval, assigning
connection desk staff, prioritizing security fast track, sending passenger
service notifications, and coordinating gate teams for late-arriving transfer
passengers.

Success means reducing missed connections by 8 percent for protected flights
during the 2026 Q3 summer peak period. The preferred output is a ranked list of
connection groups with risk level, passenger count, affected terminals, and
recommended passenger service action.
