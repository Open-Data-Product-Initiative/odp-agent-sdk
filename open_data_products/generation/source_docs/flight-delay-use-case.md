# Flight Delay Risk Monitoring

Flight Delay Risk Monitoring helps the airport operations control center detect
departures that are likely to miss their target off-block time. The main users
are duty managers, gate coordinators, airline station managers, and turnaround
supervisors.

The workflow begins three hours before scheduled departure and continues until
the aircraft leaves the stand. Users review delay risk by flight, terminal, gate,
handling agent, and delay reason category. The use case supports decisions such
as gate swaps, stand reassignment, additional ground handling staff, baggage belt
priority, and proactive passenger communication.

The use case depends on the Airport Operations Performance Data Product. It also
needs reliable timestamps from gate events, aircraft stand events, baggage
loading events, and updated estimated departure times.

Success means duty managers can identify high-risk departures earlier and reduce
reaction time by 20 percent during the summer peak period. The preferred output
is a ranked list of flights with risk level, likely reason, affected terminal,
and recommended operational action.
