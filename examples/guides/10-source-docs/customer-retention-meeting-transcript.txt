Meeting transcript: customer retention analytics data product

Participants:
- Maya, Head of Retention Analytics
- Leon, Data Platform Lead
- Priya, Lifecycle Marketing Manager
- Omar, Customer Success Operations

Maya: The retention team needs a reusable customer analytics data product. We
keep recreating customer extracts for churn review, campaign targeting, and
customer-success prioritization. The product should combine account profile,
subscription status, product usage, purchase history, support ticket volume,
campaign contacts, and churn-risk signals.

Priya: The main users are retention managers, lifecycle marketing analysts, and
customer-success leads. They need weekly segments for renewal risk, onboarding
risk, expansion opportunities, and customers who stopped using key features.

Omar: Support ticket context is important. A customer with declining product
usage and a growing unresolved ticket backlog should appear as high priority.
We also want a freshness expectation. Daily refresh is enough for weekly churn
review, but stale data over 24 hours should be flagged.

Leon: The first version will be an internal dataset published from the customer
warehouse. Access will be through governed warehouse tables and a BI semantic
model. Authentication follows the existing single sign-on and role-based access
controls. We do not have external pricing because this is an internal product.

Maya: For success metrics, the product should help reduce preventable churn and
improve campaign conversion. The team will review churn-risk coverage, segment
freshness, and the number of high-risk accounts contacted before renewal.
