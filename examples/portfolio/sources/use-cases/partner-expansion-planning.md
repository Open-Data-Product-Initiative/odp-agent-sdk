# Partner Expansion Planning

The partnerships team is preparing a quarterly planning process for regional
channel partners. They need a simple way to compare partner performance,
coverage gaps, and customer segments where partner-led selling is gaining
traction.

The use case starts from business planning rather than an existing product.
The team expects to combine partner pipeline history, account geography,
industry segments, customer health, and product adoption indicators.

The near-term decision is which partners should receive enablement resources
and which regions need additional coverage. The use case can share customer
health and account segmentation data with the retention workflow, but the
partner-specific data may need a separate product later.
