# Retention Risk Workflow

The customer success team wants a daily view of accounts that may churn before
the next renewal conversation. Today the team reviews support tickets, product
usage exports, and CRM notes manually, which means high-risk accounts are often
noticed too late.

The business objective is to improve retention by giving account managers a
prioritized list of customers that need intervention. The first workflow should
highlight accounts with falling product usage, open severity-one support cases,
negative survey comments, or renewal dates within the next 90 days.

The workflow is intended for customer success managers and revenue operations.
The expected outcome is faster outreach, fewer missed renewal risks, and a more
consistent weekly retention review.
