# Customer Health Data Product Discussion

Notes from product discussion on 2026-06-02.

The proposed product is called Customer Health Signals. It should provide
account-level health indicators for retention and renewal workflows. The first
audience is customer success managers, with revenue operations as the owner of
the operating cadence.

The product should include account identifiers, account owner, renewal date,
contract value band, product usage trend, support severity summary, NPS or
survey signal when available, and an overall churn-risk band.

The product is internal. The initial status should be proposed or draft until
the data quality checks are agreed. Daily freshness is enough for the first
version. Availability can be best effort during pilot. Pricing is not needed
for external customers, but an internal starter plan or zero-cost plan is fine
for the generated draft.

Known evidence gaps:

- exact source system names need confirmation;
- data contract fields are not finalized;
- SLA details are not committed beyond daily freshness;
- legal/license terms are internal-only and still informal.

Desired product ID: customer-health-signals
Visibility: internal
Type: dataset
Status: proposed
