# Partner Performance Product Brief

The partnerships team may need a Partner Performance Signals product, but this
is less mature than the customer health product.

The product would help regional leaders compare partner pipeline contribution,
win rates, active opportunities, coverage gaps, and customer segments. It may
also reuse customer health data to identify accounts where partner assistance
could improve retention or expansion.

This product is not ready for a complete specification. Generate a product
reference only if the evidence is strong enough, and mark uncertainty clearly.
If an ODPS product draft is created, keep it minimal and include review
warnings for missing ownership, SLA, pricing, and data quality evidence.

Desired product ID: partner-performance-signals
Visibility: internal
Type: dataset
Status: proposed
