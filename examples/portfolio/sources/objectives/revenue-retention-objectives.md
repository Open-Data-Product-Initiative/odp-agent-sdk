# Revenue Retention And Expansion Objectives

The leadership team wants the customer intelligence portfolio to support two
business objectives during the next planning cycle.

First, reduce preventable churn by helping customer success teams identify
renewal risks earlier and coordinate interventions before account health drops
too far.

Second, improve partner-led expansion planning by giving regional teams a
clearer view of partner coverage gaps, pipeline contribution, and customer
segments where partner activity can accelerate growth.

The portfolio should prioritize evidence that links use cases, market signals,
and product concepts back to these objectives.
